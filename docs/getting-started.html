Getting started with AI Vision Edge

Your cameras, watched by AI on this computer. Nothing is sent to the cloud. Follow these steps once, in order. Most people are done in 20–30 minutes.

1Install

You need: a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit, 8 GB memory or more) that can stay on, about 3 GB of free disk plus room for recordings, an internet connection during setup, and your cameras on the same router or Wi-Fi as the PC.

  1. Double-click AIVisionEdge-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway. Then click Yes when Windows asks for permission.
  3. Keep the install folder as C:\AIVisionEdge.
  4. Keep Plain-English search, vehicle colour/type and number plates ticked unless the PC is low on memory or disk.
  5. Wait while it sets up. The bar and the text under it show how much has been downloaded, the speed and the time left. On a typical connection this takes 5–15 minutes.
  6. Click Finish. The dashboard opens in your browser.
AI Vision Edge runs as a Windows service: it starts by itself when the PC turns on, even before anyone signs in, and restarts itself if anything goes wrong. You never need to "open" it, just the dashboard.
Setup failed? It's usually the internet connection. Run the installer again: it keeps everything already downloaded and continues.

2Keep this PC awake

A sleeping PC can't watch cameras.

  1. Open Settings → System → Power (on Windows 11: Power & battery) → Screen and sleep.
  2. Set When plugged in, put my device to sleep after to Never. The screen can still turn off.
  3. Keep a laptop plugged in.

It's fine to lock the PC or sign out. The cameras keep being watched.

3Create the admin account

  1. Open the dashboard: http://127.0.0.1:8000, or Start → AI Vision Edge dashboard. If it doesn't load, wait a minute and refresh: the first start is slower.
  2. The first time, it asks you to Create the admin account. Choose a username and a strong password, then click Create account.
Write the password down somewhere safe. The admin account controls everything, including other people's access.

4Add your cameras

  1. Make sure your cameras are switched on and connected to the same router or Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. In the dashboard go to Cameras and click Add cameras. It looks for cameras on your network for a few seconds.
  3. Type your cameras' Username and Password once and click Connect. Most people use the same login for every camera; if one is different, click This one has a different password under it.
  4. You'll see a live picture from each camera with a name ready (Hikvision camera 1…). Rename any you like, for example Front door, and untick any you don't want.
  5. Click Add cameras. Done.
Have a recorder (NVR/DVR)? Use the recorder's login: every camera on it is found at once.

Hikvision

  • The username is usually admin. Use the password set when the cameras were installed.
  • Check the password before clicking Connect. After a few wrong tries, Hikvision cameras lock logins for 30 minutes.
  • A brand-new camera must be activated once: the dashboard shows a link to open it in your browser and create a password.

Tapo

  1. Tapo cameras need a "camera account" first. In the Tapo app, tap the camera → ⚙ settings → Advanced settings → Camera account, and create a username and password (not your Tapo app login).
  2. Back in the dashboard click Search again, and use that username and password.

The dashboard recognises Tapo and Hikvision cameras that aren't ready yet and shows these steps for them. Battery-powered Tapo cameras can't be used. Neither can Ring, Nest, Blink, Arlo or most Wyze cameras: they only send video to their own cloud.

A phone as a camera

  1. On the phone, install Larix Broadcaster (free) and connect it to the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. In Add cameras, open Use a phone as a camera and scan the QR code with the phone (on Android, with Google Lens or any QR scanner). Allow it to open Larix and tap OK.
  3. In Larix, tap the round record button, then click My phone is streaming in the dashboard, Connect, and Add camera.
If the phone can't connect, your Wi-Fi may be set as a public network on the PC. Open Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi, click your network and set Network profile type to Private. Phones can only connect to this PC on private networks.

Adding a camera by its address

For other cameras, click Add by address and enter its stream address, for example rtsp://user:password@192.168.1.50:554/stream2 (the camera's manual lists it). Passwords are stored encrypted and never shown again.

If the router gives a camera a new address (common after a power cut), AI Vision Edge finds it again by itself within a few minutes. There's nothing to do.

Mount cameras high and angled down. Only film places you're allowed to, and put up a notice where the law requires one.

5Tell it what to watch for

On Cameras, click Rules on a camera. You'll see a still image from it. Pick a rule type (Occupancy and Privacy mask are under More rule types) and click the corners on the image:

RuleWhat it does
ZoneAlert when someone enters an area, for example the driveway at night.
LineCount people crossing, for example in and out of a shop door. It can also alert.
LoiteringAlert when someone stays in an area too long.
OccupancyAlert when too many people are inside an area.
Privacy maskBlack out an area, for example a neighbour's window. It's never analysed or saved.
  • Press Enter (or Finish) to save the shape, Backspace to undo a corner, Esc to cancel.
  • Choose Severity: High alerts go to your phone by default.
  • Under When the rule is active, choose Only at certain times for night-only rules. Overnight times such as 22:00–06:00 work.
  • To change a rule, click it and drag its corners.

Too many alerts, or people counted twice? Go to Settings → Performance and set Detection sensitivity lower. Missing people in the dark? Set it higher.

6Get alerts on your phone

  1. Install the free ntfy app on your phone (Play Store, F-Droid or App Store).
  2. In the dashboard, open Settings → Phone notifications. Click Generate to create a private topic name, tick Send alerts to my phone, and click Save.
  3. In the ntfy app, tap + and subscribe to that exact topic name.
  4. Back in the dashboard, click Send test. You should get a notification within seconds.

You'll also get a short daily summary each morning, and a warning if a camera is covered or moved, if the AI stops, or if the disk is nearly full.

Keep the topic name secret. Anyone who knows it can read your alerts.

7Recording and playback

  1. Open Settings → Recording and tick Record all cameras continuously.
  2. Set Keep recordings (days) and a Storage limit (GB). Plan on about 5–10 GB per camera per day. The oldest recordings are deleted first, and 5 GB of disk is always kept free.
  3. Watch recordings on the Playback page: pick a camera and a day, then click the timeline.

Even without continuous recording, every alert keeps a snapshot and a short clip.

8Search, vehicles and number plates

  1. Open Settings → Performance and tick Plain-English search. It needs about 0.5–0.7 GB of extra memory.
  2. On the Search page, describe what you're looking for, for example person in a red jacket or white van. Vehicles also get a colour and type you can filter by.
  3. For number plates: open Settings → Number plates and tick Read number plates on vehicles. The first time, it downloads its reading model, so the PC needs internet then. It works best with a camera aimed at a gate or entrance.
  4. Add plates to the Watchlist to get an alert when one is seen.

If you unticked search during install, these options won't work until you reinstall with it ticked.

Plates are personal data. Put up a camera notice, and set how long sightings are kept in Settings → Data retention.

9Add other people

In Settings → Users, give each person their own account. Don't share yours.

  • Operator: can watch live, review and acknowledge alerts, search and play back recordings.
  • Admin: can also change cameras, rules, settings and users.

Every sign-in and change is recorded in Settings → Audit log.

10See it from anywhere (optional)

The dashboard only answers on this PC. To use it from your phone, at home or away, use Tailscale (free). It opens no ports to the internet.

  1. Install Tailscale on this PC and on your phone, and sign in with the same account on both.
  2. On this PC, open Command Prompt and run: 
    tailscale serve --bg 8000
    It prints an address like https://my-pc.tail1234.ts.net. If it asks you to turn on HTTPS for your tailnet, open the link it shows, then run the command again.
  3. Open that address on your phone. To install it like an app: in Chrome on Android choose Add to Home screen; on iPhone, in Safari choose Share → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Paste the address into Settings → Phone notifications → Dashboard link. Alerts then get Acknowledge and Mute camera 1 h buttons, and tapping one opens the dashboard.

11Day to day

PageUse it for
OverviewToday at a glance: alerts, cameras online, people in and out.
LiveAll cameras at once.
EventsEvery alert with its snapshot and clip. Mark each one Acknowledge or False alarm. False alarms help you tune your rules.
SearchFind a person or vehicle by description, colour or plate.
PlaybackRecorded video by camera and day.
AnalyticsBusy times, counts and how well each rule performs.

Backups happen automatically every night to C:\AIVisionEdge\backups (the newest 14 are kept). To also keep a copy in OneDrive or Google Drive, ask your installer to set it up.

To silence one camera for an hour (for example while you're working in the garden), use Mute 1 h on its card. Detection and recording carry on.

12If something's wrong

ProblemTry this
Dashboard won't openWait a minute after starting the PC. If it still won't open: Start → type Services → find AI Vision Edge → Restart.
A camera shows offlineCheck its power and network cable or Wi-Fi. If the router gave it a new address, it reconnects by itself within a few minutes. Still offline after 15 minutes? Open it in the camera's own app to check it works.
Phone (Larix) can't connectSet the Wi-Fi to Private (step 4). If the PC's address changed, scan the QR code in Add cameras → Use a phone as a camera again.
No phone alertsClick Send test in Settings → Phone notifications. Check the topic name matches exactly in the ntfy app, and that the rule's severity matches Notify for.
Too many false alarmsLower Detection sensitivity, make zones smaller, or limit rules to night hours.
PC is slowIn Settings → Performance, lower Analysis speed to 2, keep Rest the AI when nothing moves on, and use cameras' low-resolution streams.
Anything elseSettings → System health shows what's not working. Logs are in C:\AIVisionEdge\storage\logs; send them to your installer.

Updating and uninstalling

To update, run the newer installer. It keeps your cameras, rules, alerts and recordings. To uninstall, use Settings → Apps → AI Vision Edge → Uninstall. Your data in C:\AIVisionEdge is kept; delete that folder too if you want everything gone.
